Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to implement AquaFlow Pro?

add
For Essential and Professional plans, you can be up and running within 24 hours. Enterprise implementations typically take 2-4 weeks depending on the complexity of your custom integrations and data migration needs.

Do drivers need special hardware?

add
No! The AquaFlow Driver App works perfectly on any standard iOS or Android smartphone. They simply download the app from the store, log in, and start receiving routes.

Can I integrate with my existing accounting software?

add
Absolutely. We have native integrations with QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage. For custom enterprise ERPs, our robust REST API allows for bidirectional syncing.
